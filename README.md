# Sales & User Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

In this project, we analyzed sales and user behavior in an online store. The main goal was to understand where revenue comes from, how users interact with the website, and how the business can improve its results.

## Project Tasks

- Analyze sales by region and product categories.
- Explore revenue from different traffic channels.
- Compare user activity across devices.
- Analyze user activity over time.
- Compare user groups using statistical tests.
- Explore the relationship between sessions and revenue.
- Create business recommendations based on the results.

## Key Findings

1. **Sales by Region:** America generated the highest revenue — approximately $17.67 million. Asia generated $7.60 million, and Europe generated $5.93 million.

2. **Devices:** Desktop devices accounted for 58.5% of sessions, while mobile devices accounted for 39.3%.

3. **Traffic Channels:** Organic Search generated $11.43 million, or 35.8% of total revenue.

4. **User Activity:** The highest number of sessions was recorded on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Activity was lower on weekends.

5. **Statistical Analysis:** We found significant differences in daily sessions between traffic channels. However, there was no significant difference in revenue per session between verified and non-verified users.

6. **Correlation Analysis:** There was a strong positive relationship between sessions and revenue. The Pearson correlation was 0.931, and the Spearman correlation was 0.904.

## Business Recommendations

- Continue developing key markets and explore other regions.
- Improve the mobile website and make the checkout process easier.
- Invest in SEO to increase organic traffic.
- Plan marketing campaigns based on user activity.
- Analyze popular product categories and find ways to increase the average order value.

## Important Notes

- The guest user group also includes users with missing verification status.
- The last four days were excluded from the daily revenue analysis because sales data was not available.

## Conclusion

This project helped us identify the main revenue sources, understand user behavior, and find opportunities for business growth. The results can help the company make better data-driven decisions.
